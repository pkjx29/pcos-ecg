## What this means for the analysis

- **Labels are self-reported.** Some "PCOS" or "control" labels may be wrong, because many
  people with PCOS are undiagnosed. Any score measures agreement with self-report, not
  with a clinical diagnosis.
- **30 participants is a pilot.** Cross-validated scores will vary a lot between folds,
  and the results need confirming in the larger, ethics-reviewed study.
- **Kardia is a consumer device.** Its recordings are short and usually single-lead, so
  morphology features (PR, QT, T-wave) are automatic single-lead estimates.
